Keys

Click one and press a key; Backspace leaves it without one, Esc keeps it as it was.

Mouse

Left drag: orbit, or drag a handle. Right drag: pan. Wheel: zoom (while flying: speed). Hold Shift or Ctrl while turning a handle for 22.5 or 1 degree steps.

How connectors run longer

The blue side slides away from the red side as one block. Copies of the section in the white box fill the gap (tinted green), and what is left over, less than one copy, continues as plain wall. The white box should be one whole repeat, from light to light, with both ends on plain wall. The yellow box is all of it that could be taken out; dragging the slider below zero shows that.
